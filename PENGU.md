## Features

* 🏆 **Virtual ranks (Iron to Challenger)**: Set custom ranks, divisions, LP, W/L, and banners for yourself, your alts, and your friends across profiles, hovercards, and lobbies.
* 📈 **Live LP & End-of-game screens**: Automatically updates your virtual LP, promotions, demotions, W/L counter, and promotion ceremonies after ranked games.
* 📊 **Leaderboards**: Places virtual ranks accurately in the friends list and live Master/GM/Challenger ladders.
* 🏷️ **Name spoofer**: Replace any Riot ID (`Name#TAG`) across profiles, chat, friends list, leaderboards, and lobbies.
* 📋 **Copycat**: Clone any player's full profile (icon, level, banners, tokens, masteries, ranks, Riot ID) onto yours by entering their `Name#TAG`.
* 🔄 **Friend sharing**: Sync your virtual rank, spoofed name, and alts with friends who also use the plugin (with an option to override their values).

## Install

1. Download `RankSpoofer.js` from the latest release.
2. Drop it into your Pengu Loader `plugins` folder.
3. Restart the League client (works in English and French).

## Settings

Press `Ctrl + K` in the client to open the command bar and select **Configure Virtual Ranks and Profiles**:

* **Accounts**: Manage your main account, alts, and friends (rank, LP, W/L, banner, spoofed name, and friend overrides).
* **Copycat**: Enter a `Name#TAG` to copy a profile or stop an active copy.
* **Display**: Toggle individual visual modules (profiles, hovercards, end-of-game screen, leaderboards, name spoofer).
* **Sharing**: Configure sending and receiving ranks/alts with friends.
* **Advanced**: Adjust Master+ LP cutoffs (auto or manual) and reload the UI.
